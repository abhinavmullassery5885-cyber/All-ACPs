My Outdoor Activity Cards

This page shows some fun and healthy activities that can be done outdoors. Each card has its own activity and time.

Go to the Park

Visit the park to enjoy fresh air, walk around, and spend some relaxing time outdoors.

Time: 30 minutes

Have Rest

Take some quiet time to relax your body and recharge after a busy day.

Time: 20 minutes

Play Outdoor Games

Play fun outdoor games with friends to stay active, have fun, and enjoy the fresh air.

Time: 30 minutes

Go for a Walk

Take a peaceful walk outside and enjoy the surroundings while staying active.

Time: 25 minutes

Ride a Bicycle

Ride your bicycle in a safe outdoor area and enjoy some fresh air and exercise.

Time: 20 minutes

Relax Outside

Sit outside, enjoy nature, and take a break from screens and busy activities.

Time: 15 minutes

Activity Tip: Spend time outdoors, stay active, have fun, and remember to give yourself enough time to rest.
